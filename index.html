About

I started out as a print designer and drifted into the browser because it was the only medium that pushed back. These days I spend most of my time on component libraries, data-dense screens and the small interaction details that decide whether a product feels trustworthy.

I work close to the platform. No build step unless the team needs one, no abstractions I cannot explain, and no animation that fights the user. When I am not writing CSS I am teaching it, usually to designers who are tired of waiting for tickets to close.
